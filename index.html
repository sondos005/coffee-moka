Our Story

Moka & Co. began in a converted bicycle repair shop on Rue du Faubourg, with a single espresso machine and a conviction that great coffee deserves an unhurried moment.

We source exclusively from small farms we visit annually — places where the altitude, the soil, and the pickers' hands shape a cup no roast profile can fully predict. That unpredictability is what we love.

Our roastery is three blocks away. Beans arrive green and leave within 72 hours of roasting. No warehousing, no compromise.
